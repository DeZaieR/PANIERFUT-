Frequently asked questions

What you need to know.

Are the videos filmed on site?

No. Everything is produced remotely from the photos, videos and materials you send, and generated with artificial intelligence.

Is the vehicle or product shown accurate?

The generated images are advertising creations. Visual features (colour, wheels, options, product details) are checked against your photos, and every shot is submitted for your approval before delivery.

Which formats will I receive?

Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, horizontal 16:9 for your website and YouTube, square 1:1 for social media. The formats included depend on the offer and are confirmed in the quote.

Are the UGC avatars real people?

No. They are AI-generated and used as an advertising format, never to fake a review or customer testimonial.

How do I send my photos?

Add a photo directly in the form, or a download link for a batch. If needed, we explain how to send us your files after your request.

Do you offer monthly support?

Yes, especially for garages and dealerships with several vehicles: volume, pace and formats are defined together, then confirmed in the quote.
