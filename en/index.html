Frequently asked questions

Your questions. Our answers.

Another question? Write to us

Are the property’s features respected?

Yes. The source photo guides the work: walls, openings, floors, perspective and volumes are preserved. Every image is checked and you approve it before any publication. A non-compliant image is adjusted or discarded.

How long does it take?

2 to 3 business days as a guide, after approval of the photos and brief. Timing depends on volume and complexity and is confirmed in the quote.

Which adjustments are included?

Each offer includes one round of adjustments within the approved brief (one round per batch for the Partner agency offer). A complete change of direction is quoted separately.

Which photos can I send?

One JPG, PNG or WebP photo up to 10 MB via the form, or a download link for several photos. A sharp, bright photo works best: if a photo is too dark, blurry or cluttered, we tell you before we start.

How do I label virtual staging in a listing?

Add a visible “Virtually staged” (or “Renovation simulation”) label to the listing and the images. The furniture and decoration are virtual and not included in the sale. Keep the original photo too, where possible.

How does the free trial work?

One free photo per new agency, with no commitment and no charge. Send your photo and your goal: we confirm whether it can be processed.

What usage rights are included?

The authorised media and the scope of rights are set out in the quote. You must hold the rights to the photos you send.

How are the videos made?

They are short edits (15 to 30 seconds) from your photos, with a vertical version. They are not filmed tours, and any virtual staging stays clearly labelled.
